Take a Pen and Paper

What follows is a series of questions drawn from The Great and Wondrous Work — an old work of self-examination, ages old and still unfulfilled.

  • Find a quiet place, conducive to contemplation.
  • Have a pen and paper, or a file, folder, or notepad, to hand.
  • Write each question down, and your answer to it.
  • Explore as fully as you need. Sometimes a question is best left alone, and returned to in time.
  • This is all for you, and your development. Go at your own pace.
Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.
